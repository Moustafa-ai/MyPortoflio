Profile

Over the years I've mastered a suite of powerful GIS tools — ArcGIS Pro 3, ArcGIS 10.x, QGIS 3.x, MapInfo, FME, ERDAS Imagine and ENVI — using them to analyze, manage and visualize spatial data across dozens of projects for international clients.

My work spans nearly every stage of the spatial data pipeline: building and updating GIS databases, high-quality georeferencing and digitizing, cartography, spatial and suitability analysis, and shipping the result as an interactive web map.

  • ArcGIS & QGIS project delivery
  • Building and updating GIS databases
  • High-quality georeferencing & digitizing of maps
  • Cartography and digital mapping
  • Network and time-series analysis
  • DEM derivatives — contour lines, hillshade
  • Data cleaning, analysis & processing with Python
  • Interactive maps — Google My Maps, Mapbox GL JS, Leaflet JS
